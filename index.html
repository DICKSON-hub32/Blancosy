About Us

Blancosy LTD is a waste recycling and product manufacturing company dedicated to creating sustainable solutions for Kenya’s waste management challenges. The company produces eco-friendly products such as Ecomakaa, a charcoal alternative made from recycled waste paper, and ECO-JIKO, an energy-efficient stove designed to work effectively with Ecomakaa. These products serve households, hotels, and community organizations that seek cleaner, more sustainable energy sources and waste management solutions. Some of our major impacts are;

  • Environmental Conservation – Successfully recycled over 10,000 tons of waste annually
  • Youth & Women Empowerment – By creating 100+ jobs
  • Community Education & Awareness

Blancosy Ltd reaches its customers through direct sales, partnerships with local vendors, and collaborations with environmental organizations, ensuring widespread access to its products. By offering affordable, sustainable options that diminish reliance on traditional charcoal and promote cleaner air, the company tackles significant environmental and health challenges in the community. The founder’s motivation is to create meaningful change through responsible waste management, reducing pollution while empowering communities to embrace sustainable practices.

Who We Are

Blancosy LTD is committed to sustainable waste management by transforming waste into valuable resources. Through innovative recycling techniques and environmental awareness programs, we reduce pollution, conserve resources, and create employment opportunities for youth and women.

Our Mission

To transform waste into valuable resources while promoting a cleaner, more sustainable environment. We empower communities through education, job creation, and circular economy practices, turning environmental challenges into opportunities for growth and impact.

Our Vision

To empower young people as leaders of environmental sustainability. We envision a future where communities thrive in harmony with nature, fostering a deep connection to the planet and each other. Through education, innovation, and action, we inspire the next generation to become proactive stewards of the Earth, driving lasting positive change.
