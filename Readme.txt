
Blancosy Website 🌍♻️
Description:
This is the official website for Blancosy, a Kenyan startup dedicated to solid waste management, environmental sustainability, and youth empowerment. The website highlights our initiatives, products, services, and opportunities for individuals and organizations to get involved in creating a cleaner and greener future.

Features:
✅ Home – Overview of Blancosy and our mission.
✅ About Us – Our vision, mission, and impact in waste management and sustainability.
✅ Resources – Educational materials and guides on eco-friendly practices.
✅ Get Involved – Ways to contribute to our cause.
✅ Our Team – Meet the passionate people behind Blancosy.
✅ Our Partners – Organizations and companies supporting our mission.
✅ Our Services – Waste collection, recycling, river cleanup, and community awareness.
✅ Our Products – EcoMakaa, EcoJiko, and plastic raw materials.
✅ Portfolio – A showcase of past projects and achievements.
✅ Contact Us – Reach out to us, including a Google Map location of our offices.

Tech Stack:
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript (Bootstrap for styling)
Backend: (Optional, if needed later) Node.js, Django, or PHP
Database: (If applicable) MySQL, PostgreSQL, Firebase
Hosting: GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel
